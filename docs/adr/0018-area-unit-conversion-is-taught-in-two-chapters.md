# Area-Unit conversion is taught in two Chapters

Ułamki Dziesiętne T120 "Jednostki" had no Level for area Units, while Geometria T130 "Pole trójkąta" already declares `expected_units: ["mm²", "cm²", "dm²"]` and ADR-0005 grades `0,0024 m²` Correct for `24 cm²` — both assuming a Student who can already convert. The adopted Geometria Topic list reserves its own Topic for the same skill (Geometria T120 "Jednostki pola i ich zamiana", klasa 5–6, `PP IV–VI XI.4`), but that Topic sits behind the whole drawing layer (#207) and is unbuilt. We decided to teach area-Unit conversion in **both** Chapters rather than wait for one or delete the other, and to write the boundary down here so the duplication is read as deliberate (#327).

**What each side owns.** Ułamki Dziesiętne T120 L5 "Jednostki pola" owns the *arithmetic*: a bare-number `Zamień: 24 cm² = ___ dm²`, no figure, no `expected_units` (per the #286 amendment to ADR-0005, the Level names the target Unit, so the answer is a bare number). Its single teaching point is that the conversion factor squares — `1 cm = 10 mm` but `1 cm² = 100 mm²` — which is why its distinguishing Trap, `uses_the_length_factor_for_area_units`, offers the length factor instead.

Geometria T120, when it is built, owns the same conversion *as measurement*, and must differ from the Ułamki Dziesiętne Level on all three counts: it draws a figure where one helps (the research note sketches a square grid subdividing `1 dm²` into 100 `cm²` cells), its answers carry their Unit and it declares `expected_units`, and its questions turn on area-versus-length reasoning rather than on the factor alone. A Geometria Level that reduces to a bare-number `Zamień:` line belongs in Ułamki Dziesiętne instead.

**Considered options:** Putting area conversion only in Ułamki Dziesiętne (rejected — it abandons a Topic the podstawa programowa places in Geometria, and loses the figure that makes the squared factor visible); leaving it to Geometria T120 alone (rejected — T130 grades area answers today, so the gap would stay open for as long as the drawing layer takes); a two-step area Level alongside the adjacent one (rejected — by the time a Student chains `cm²`→`m²` the squared factor is taught, and the chaining itself is already Ułamki Dziesiętne T120 L4 "Podwójna zamiana długości").

**Consequences:** `mm² cm² dm² m²` appear in questions in two Chapters, and a change to the Unit table or to how Units are written in prose (`backend/core/utils.py`, #286) touches both. `a` and `ha` are in the Ułamki Dziesiętne Level's pool but `km²` is not: `ha`↔`km²` is the same ×100 step wearing a larger number, and admitting it drags in `m²`↔`km²` at ×1 000 000.
