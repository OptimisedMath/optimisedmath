# Fraction and decimal Chapters never serve a negative number, Traps included

Ułamki Zwykłe and Ułamki Dziesiętne come before negative numbers in the curriculum, so a Student working these Chapters has no rule that produces one. No option a Problem in these Chapters serves — the correct answer, a Trap, or a Filler — may be negative. A Trap whose wrong rule would compute a negative value is not the answer that Student gives, because they cannot reach it (#266).

This reverses the exemption `tests/test_no_negative_answers.py` used to grant Traps — *"a Student who multiplies before squaring may well land on a negative number, and hiding that would remove the Trap."* That reasoning assumed the Student could land there. They cannot, and the exemption had a visible cost: on the `Kolejność działań` Boss Level both wrong-order Traps were negative on every Problem, so a Student in Radio mode could discard two of four options without doing any arithmetic.

**Considered options:** Keeping the exemption and recording it (rejected — it rests on an answer these Students do not produce, and leaves the Boss Level a two-way guess); forbidding negatives only where every wrong option is negative (rejected — on the Boss Level one Trap is always positive, so that rule never fires); hard-forbidding negatives across the whole Curriculum (rejected — the premise is about where these two Chapters sit, and a later Chapter on negative numbers or coordinates must be free to serve them); each generator passing `None` for a Trap that would be negative (rejected — the same branch in every template, and it cannot catch a Filler invented to pad a slot); a Chapter-level YAML switch (rejected — generators are called with no arguments, so the switch could only act after options are chosen, which would move Trap selection and Filler padding out of `build_problem_dict`).

**Consequences:** `build_problem_dict` does not offer a negative option — Trap or Filler — unless the call passes `allow_negative_options=True`. The default suits every Chapter that exists today; a Topic that wants negative Traps opts in per call, so the mechanism does not prefer one Chapter's premise over another's. A Trap skipped for being negative frees its slot for the next Trap in declaration order (ADR-0008). The negative-answer sweep covers every option in Ułamki Zwykłe and Ułamki Dziesiętne, not only the correct answer, which is what catches a fraction generator opting in by mistake.

**Amended by #272 / ADR-0009.** "When no Trap is left to take it, the Problem is served with fewer options" no longer holds: a slot a negative Trap frees is filled by a Filler like any other empty slot, whatever emptied it. A Filler itself is still never negative here — the shape and collision rules in ADR-0009 apply `allow_negative_options` exactly as a Trap does.
